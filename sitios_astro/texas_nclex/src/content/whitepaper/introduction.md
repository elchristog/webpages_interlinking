# Whitepaper: Buio - Revolutionizing Crypto Solutions

## Introduction to the Project

At Buio, we aim to solve the fundamental problem of scalability and security in blockchain transactions. Traditional blockchain systems often face challenges with speed and efficiency, limiting their widespread adoption. Our platform leverages cutting-edge technologies to offer faster transaction times, lower fees, and enhanced security, providing an ideal solution for decentralized applications (dApps) and digital assets.

## Technical Details

Buio utilizes a hybrid blockchain model that combines Proof of Stake (PoS) and Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) to ensure both security and scalability. Our blockchain is built on a high-performance architecture, allowing for faster transaction throughput and reduced latency. Smart contracts are used to automate processes, ensuring that users can interact with decentralized applications seamlessly and securely.

## Tokenomics

The Buio token (BUIO) is an ERC-20 token with a total supply of 1 billion tokens. These tokens are distributed as follows:
* 40% for public and private sales
* 20% for staking rewards
* 15% for the development team
* 10% for strategic partnerships
* 10% for community incentives
* 5% for legal and compliance reserves

BUIO tokens can be used for transaction fees, staking rewards, governance voting, and participating in ecosystem incentives.
