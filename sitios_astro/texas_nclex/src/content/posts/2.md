Web3 is redefining authentication with **decentralized identity (DID)**, allowing users to control their digital identity without intermediaries.

## What is Decentralized Identity?

Decentralized Identity (DID) is a new paradigm where users manage their own identity through **blockchain-based credentials** rather than relying on a third party like Google or Facebook.

### Key benefits:

- **User sovereignty**: Own and control your identity.
- **Privacy-first**: Authenticate without sharing unnecessary personal data.
- **Interoperability**: Use one identity across multiple Web3 applications.

With **DIDs and Verifiable Credentials**, users can log into platforms using cryptographic proofs instead of passwords, making authentication more secure and private.
