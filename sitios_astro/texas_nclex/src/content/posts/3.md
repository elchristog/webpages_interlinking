Web3 wallets are evolving beyond simple key pairs. **Smart contract wallets** enable better security, usability, and gas efficiency.

## Why Smart Contract Wallets?

Unlike traditional wallets (EOA), smart contract wallets offer:

- **Gasless Transactions**: Users can pay gas fees in any token or use sponsored transactions.
- **Multi-signature Security**: Protect assets with multiple approvers.
- **Account Recovery**: Unlike seed phrases, smart wallets can have social recovery features.

Popular smart contract wallets like **Argent** and **Safe (Gnosis Safe)** are leading the way in making crypto wallets more user-friendly.
