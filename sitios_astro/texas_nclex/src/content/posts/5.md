**Soulbound Tokens (SBTs)** are **non-transferable NFTs** that represent credentials, achievements, or reputation on-chain.

## How SBTs Work

Unlike regular NFTs, **SBTs cannot be sold or transferred**, making them ideal for:

- **On-chain resumes** (e.g., proof of education, work experience).
- **Reputation systems** in DAOs.
- **Access control** (e.g., exclusive event passes).

Vitalik Buterin introduced the concept of SBTs in 2022, and since then, projects have been exploring how they can revolutionize **decentralized identity**.
